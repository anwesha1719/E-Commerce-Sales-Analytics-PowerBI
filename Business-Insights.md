# Business Insights & Recommendations

## Business Insights

### 1. Revenue Performance
Electronics is the strongest product category by revenue, indicating strong customer demand for electronic products.

**Recommendation:** Maintain sufficient inventory and focus marketing efforts on high-performing categories.

### 2. Customer Revenue Contribution
Pune contributes the highest customer revenue among the cities shown in the dashboard.

**Recommendation:** Use targeted offers and retention campaigns for customers in high-revenue cities.

### 3. Customer Retention
The dashboard shows a high customer repeat rate of approximately 95%.

**Recommendation:** Strengthen loyalty programs and personalized offers to encourage repeat purchases.

### 4. Order Delivery Performance
Out of 500 total orders, 349 were delivered, giving a delivery rate of 69.8%.

**Recommendation:** Investigate delivery delays and unsuccessful deliveries to improve fulfillment performance.

### 5. Order Cancellation
There were 53 cancelled orders out of 500 total orders, resulting in a cancellation rate of 10.6%.

**Recommendation:** Analyze cancellation reasons and improve order confirmation and fulfillment processes.

### 6. Payment Methods
Net Banking contributes strongly to order volume and revenue among the payment methods shown.

**Recommendation:** Maintain reliable payment processing while analyzing other payment methods for improvement opportunities.
